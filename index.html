How we work

A disciplined, PMBOK-aligned approach, now driven by AI

  1. AssessConfirm condition, scope, contract requirements and constraints.
  2. PlanSet the WBS, AFE budget, schedule, risk register and procurement plan.
  3. ExecuteControl cost, schedule, vendors and quality with weekly reporting.
  4. DeliverCommission, pass acceptance testing and hand over a contract-ready rig.

AI-driven project management

AI does the checking. A senior project manager makes the decisions.

Our project management approach is now supported and driven by AI. AI tools read the full data set every week: every purchase order line, every cost entry and every schedule activity. Nothing is sampled and nothing waits for month-end.

The method stays the same: PMBOK-aligned controls, AFE governance and clear accountability. AI makes it faster and more complete, so problems surface weeks earlier and reports arrive on time.
