How we work

Four steps, PMBOK-aligned, checked by AI every week

  1. AssessConfirm the rig's condition, the scope, the contract terms and the constraints.
  2. PlanSet the WBS, AFE budget, schedule, risk register and procurement plan before work starts.
  3. ExecuteControl cost, schedule, vendors and quality. Report every week.
  4. DeliverCommission, pass acceptance tests and hand over a rig ready for its contract.

AI-driven project management

AI does the checking. A senior project manager makes the decisions.

AI reads the full project data every week: every purchase order line, every cost entry, every schedule activity. Nothing is sampled. Nothing waits for month-end.

The method does not change: PMBOK-aligned controls, AFE governance and one accountable lead. AI makes the checking faster and complete, so problems show up weeks earlier.
