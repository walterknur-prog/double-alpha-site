Deepwater activity in Nigeria, Angola and Namibia is pushing West African floater demand toward full utilization. This outlook projects rig demand, utilization and dayrates through 2030 and looks at how contractors are positioned.

## Demand outlook

| Year | Floaters required | Utilization |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 31 active | 85% |
| 2025 | 33 | 87% |
| 2026 | 37 | 3–4 more rigs than today |
| 2027 | 39+ | Shortfall without reactivations |

## Key points

- Nigeria is expected to double to 4 rigs by 2025; Angola holds about 7 rigs long term; Namibia could add 3 to 5 by the late 2020s.
- Dayrates are in the low-to-mid $400k/day range, with $500k/day possible by 2026.
- Contractors able to reactivate stacked assets at scale stand to capture premium returns.
